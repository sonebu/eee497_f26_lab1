EEE 497 - Lab 01
Recover audio from an IQ capture

You are given the complex baseband IQ dump file: capture.iq. Your task is to recover the audio inside that dump.

The following information are provided:
- type:         unsigned 8-bit
- byte layout:  interleaved I, Q, I, Q, ...
- fs:           512000 Hz
- conversion:   (x - 127) / 128

Your task:
1. Load the IQ.
2. Find the signal that carries the audio in the spectrum. Report its estimated IF (Hz) and occupied bandwidth.
3. Design and apply a bandpass filter to isolate the signal (FIR, IIR, etc., your design).
4. Identify the signal modulation inside that band. 
5. Up/downconvert it to (complex) baseband, demodulate with a chain you implement, low-pass, decimate to an audio rate etc.
6. Write a WAV containing the audio.

Deliverables are code that reads the IQ and processes it, the output WAV, and a lab report that explains your work in detail.
